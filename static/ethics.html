Data ethics, provenance, and limitations

This page summarizes the project’s governing policy. It describes commitments and known limitations; it is not a claim that every control is already operational.

What a record means

  • Source origin identifies where evidence came from. Government-sourced does not mean current, complete, accurate, project-approved, or independently verified.
  • Project-reviewed and project-approved are scoped decisions about specified evidence and releases. They are not guarantees of truth.
  • Project-published means the project made a record available in a named release or profile. Publication and approval are separate properties.
  • Observation dates describe retained observations, not guaranteed opening, operating, or closure dates.

Locations and uncertainty

Coordinates may be exact, city-level, approximate, unavailable, unresolved, or restricted. A registered office or geocoder match is not by itself evidence of an operating facility. We do not use disappearance from a source as proof of closure, and we do not publish guessed replacement points.

Data boundaries

Candidate, staged, quarantined, and raw evidence are not public data. Public responses are intended to use eligible records from a promoted release. Internal artifacts and source evidence may have different access and retention rules.

Corrections and privacy/location requests

To report an incorrect location, residential/private exposure, harmful identifying detail, or other data concern, email untileverycageproject@protonmail.com. For a location-exposure concern, use the subject “Privacy/location removal” and do not post sensitive evidence publicly.

Requests are assessed under the project policy, including prompt suppression of credible exposure concerns, correction or removal review, and propagation through project-controlled public outputs where applicable. The project cannot guarantee recall of independent copies.

Policy and limitations

Read the full ETHICS.md policy in the repository. The policy requires honest uncertainty, source traceability, privacy screening, correction/removal processes, and accountable publication decisions. This website does not claim legal immunity, absence of logging, or completed protections that have not been audited.
