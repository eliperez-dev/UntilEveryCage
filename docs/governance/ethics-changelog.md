# Ethics policy changelog

Governing document: [ETHICS.md](../ETHICS.md). The policy remains version 1.0 because it has not yet been publicly published. The entries below are drafting history within that initial version, not separate published releases. Repository commits provide exact text history. After initial publication, record subsequent amendments by version and update the [summary](../ETHICS-SUMMARY.md) when meaning changes. No entry establishes completed technical implementation.

## Version 1.0 drafting history — 2026-09-12

### Source, review, and project authority clarification

- Defined government-sourced, community-submitted, community-reviewed/unreviewed, project-reviewed, project-approved, and project-published explicitly as separate properties.
- Clarified that community review is not project review or approval, publication can include explicitly unreviewed claims, and curated releases may approve evidence from different origins.
- Replaced ambiguous “official” terminology in active claim policy and added migration/labeling test tasks. Policy remains unpublished version 1.0.
- Approval reference: developer's explicit instruction to distinguish project publication/approval, community review states, and government sources.

### Explicit unreviewed-community access clarification

- Following the developer's clarification, permit explicit public queries of privacy/abuse-screened claims without requiring factual review. Require prominent warnings, separate results/counts, and status metadata across APIs and exports.
- Keep unscreened, rejected, and restricted content non-public. Factual review and publication safety are separate decisions; opt-in never bypasses removal protections.
- Updated the summary, submission plan, architecture guidance, agent instructions, and open implementation checklist. Policy remains unpublished version 1.0.
- Approval reference: developer's explicit project-conversation direction that unreviewed submissions should be publicly queryable with unmistakable context.

### Governance, privacy, and presentation refinement

- Added visitor privacy requirements and a requirement to audit real hosting, logging, and provider behavior before making public claims.
- Defined maintainer-absence handling: pause new publication needing approval, preserve eligible operation, and permit authorized urgent suppression. No backup or counsel relationship is assumed.
- Added careful legal-demand and preservation handling, avoiding blanket refusal, automatic disclosure, and automatic public disclosure of demands.
- Retained the narrow professional-role naming exception with explicit relevance review; contextualized the Denmark retail example.
- Added the plain-language summary, amendment process, and implementation tasks for privacy audits, governance, vulnerability reporting, and release integrity/signing.
- Rationale: make the policy useful to different audiences and realistic for a small project without promising unverified protection.
- Approval reference: developer's explicit instruction in the project conversation to implement the agreed review recommendations. No independent legal review is asserted.

### Removal and credibility refinement

- Added controlled removal exceptions to default append-only history, a residential/address/coordinate removal process, and restrictions across public outputs, reimports, and restores.
- Distinguished process commitments from factual accuracy guarantees and clarified maintainer responsibilities.
- Rationale: reconcile evidence preservation with protection of individuals and correction of harmful or inaccurate information.
- Approval reference: developer's explicit instruction in the project conversation to update the ethics policy after review.
