# Our ethics, simply

Until Every Cage documents animal exploitation through inspectable evidence. These are our rules for building and publishing the project. The [full ethics policy](ETHICS.md), version 1.0 and not yet publicly published, is authoritative; this summary does not replace it.

1. **Show the evidence.** Identify sources and known dates. Keep government statements, project interpretations, geocoding, and submitted claims distinguishable.
2. **Be honest about uncertainty.** Government records can be wrong or outdated. We commit to a traceable process, not guaranteed truth, completeness, or exact locations.
3. **Document facilities without exposing private lives.** Protect workers, residents, and other private individuals. Residential/private addresses, wrong-property locations, and harmful identifying information have an explicit review and removal path. Relevant professional-role references require exceptional review.
4. **Correct mistakes and prevent re-exposure.** Review concerns independently of whether the government has corrected its record. Restrictions must cover maps, APIs, downloads, old releases, and subsequent imports—not just hide a pin.
5. **Keep accountable history, with controlled removal.** Ordinary changes create new evidence. Sensitive material can be restricted or removed through a documented process that does not copy it into the audit log.
6. **Respect visitors' privacy.** Minimize logs and location collection, avoid behavioral tracking, and explain external services. Claim only protections verified in the deployed system.

**Community claims:** You may explicitly explore privacy/abuse-screened submissions that have not been factually reviewed. They must be prominently labeled “Unreviewed community claim — not verified by Until Every Cage,” separate from the default curated project dataset, and excluded from default searches and exports. Screening does not verify accuracy. Raw unscreened or restricted submissions stay private, and opting in never overrides location-removal protections.

**Read the labels separately:** Government-sourced tells you where evidence came from. Community-reviewed/unreviewed describes factual review by community contributors; project-reviewed identifies a separate project review. Project-approved means an authorized maintainer approved that version for a curated release. Project-published means it is available through a named profile—even an unreviewed-community profile. Publication is not factual approval, community review does not confer project approval, and none guarantees truth.

## Report a concern privately

Email **untileverycageproject@protonmail.com** with a record link/ID, the concern, and the requested correction or removal. For address or coordinate exposure, use “Privacy/location removal” in the subject. You do not need to obtain an upstream government correction first. Avoid putting private evidence in public issues or sending unnecessary identity documents. This is not an emergency-response service.

## For contributors and agents

Read the full policy before changing data, retention, publication, or visitor-data handling. Escalate sensitive judgments to the responsible maintainer. New publication needing human approval pauses when no authorized reviewer is available; eligible existing releases and compliant acquisition can continue. Urgent suppression follows the removal procedure.

**Implementation status:** These are binding requirements, not confirmation that all controls exist today. The [implementation checklist](governance/policy-implementation-todo.md) records open work. The future public page must describe actual verified behavior. See the [changelog](governance/ethics-changelog.md) for policy amendments.
